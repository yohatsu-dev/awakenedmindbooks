Overthinking and Rumination

Overthinking does not solve problems: it generates an endless simulation that drains the energy to live. The Buddha provided sequential protocols to displace intrusive thought patterns.

1. Understanding the pattern

How the Tibetan contemplative tradition analyzes the root of this disturbance and why fighting it often reinforces the cycle.

2. The transformative technology

The practical procedure to observe the mind in action, interrupt automatic reactivity, and anchor attention in the present moment.

3. Step-by-step practice

A 5 to 10-minute applicable exercise for moments of tension or daily routine.
