“Why call them technologies?

Because that is closer to how the tradition treated them. A figure like Tara or Manjushri was never only an image to admire; it was a set of instructions, a way of doing something inside your own head with a result you could check.

This series keeps the method in front and the meaning behind it. Each book takes one recurring problem of the mind, strips the practice down to what you actually do, and leaves the rest for the reader who wants to go further.
