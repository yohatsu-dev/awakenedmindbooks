From the book: Hey Mr. Buddha, Make Me Stop Overthinking

21 Buddhist practices to quiet anxiety, break the loop, and get on with your life.

“It was a Thursday, and Clara had spent thirty minutes improving a conversation with her ex — a conversation that had ended four days earlier and was somehow still receiving updates. She got up to deal with the sink. Washed the bowl. Washed the pan. Became strangely interested in a stain on the bottom of the pan. Four minutes in, she noticed her ex had left the building. Not because she won, and not because she settled anything. He was evicted by a saucepan.

So the Buddha calls in a carpenter: a skilled carpenter knocks a coarse peg out of a hole by driving a finer one into the same spot. No struggle, no acceptance, no leaf floating down a river, no music in the background. There is a hole with a peg in it, and to get one out you drive another in. How many holes does attention have? One. That is very bad news for the committee.”
