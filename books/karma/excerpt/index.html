From the book: That's Karma, Man!

Stop freaking out, accept the obvious, and learn to plant better what you want to harvest.

“Karma is not the universe waking up at three thirty in the morning to arrange a sequence of domestic humiliations. There is no old man with a white beard logging your failures on a clipboard, working out the exact angle to drop the LEGO brick where your bare foot will land. There is no cosmic accountant. There is no revenge of fate.

The dog on the rug, the bread turned to charcoal, the gum in the child's hair: that is just a bad morning. There is no karma in any of it. The karma comes afterwards, in what she does with the bad morning. That is where the seed goes in. And she is planting right now, without noticing.”
